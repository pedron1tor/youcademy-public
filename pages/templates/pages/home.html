{% custom_trans "Why should I choose you?" %}

{% custom_trans "We are the only provider in the Netherlands that is at the forefront of educational innovation while basing everything on scientific research. Furthermore, with us, you're not just a 'customer'. We prefer to see you as a partner. We are not a standard solution: teachers tell us what challenges they face, and based on that, we further develop the platform." %}

{% custom_trans "Who are the people behind YouCademy?" %}

{% custom_trans "The two founders are Tjalling van der Schaar and Pedro Nieto. In 2021, they were named among the 100 most promising 'changemakers' in the world by Forbes Magazine and are sponsored by the ex-CEO of Google, who pays for their education and associated costs (Rise Scholarship). In return for this sponsorship, they have both promised to use everything they receive to make this world a better place. We are further assisted by advisors, including the co-founder of Booking.com." %}

{% custom_trans "What about privacy?" %}

{% custom_trans "We have signed contracts with all partners we work with to keep the website and AI models online. In short, all data we collect remains private. The data we share with third parties (who are contractually obligated to keep it private) is anonymized. The data we share can never be traced back to a specific person or even a specific school. For a detailed explanation per country, please refer to our privacy policy." %}

{% custom_trans "Do you offer free trial periods with all features?" %}

{% custom_trans "Individual teachers can try YouCademy for free for one month with a usage limit on the features we offer. For school subscriptions, we are more flexible and can offer so-called pilots with the full version of the platform to see if it is suitable for your school. If we offered more free trial periods, we would have to make the monthly price for users more expensive to cover the costs, which is why we have chosen this balance." %}

{% custom_trans "How do you know that YouCademy works?" %}

{% custom_trans "We base all our features on scientific research paired with teacher input. Furthermore, we have dynamic machine learning models that continuously adapt the learning material based on student outcomes. Additionally, we internally track all data to ensure that YouCademy always delivers the promised educational value! We are currently setting up studies at partner schools to quantify the precise improvements of students using YouCademy." %}

{% custom_trans "How did you calculate the price?" %}

{% custom_trans "We understand that school budgets are not huge. On the other hand, such forms of personalization require a lot of computing power. We didn't start this to make a big profit, and by breaking even, we can offer the best services at the lowest price." %}
